# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good intent, criticize ideas rather than people, and respect privacy and licensing boundaries. Maintainers may remove abusive, discriminatory, threatening, or persistently disruptive contributions and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

No private reporting contact is currently listed in this repository. Please avoid posting personal or sensitive details in public discussions. This document will be updated with contact information when that channel is available.
